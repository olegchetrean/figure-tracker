        About this site & disclaimers

        ⚠ Read before sharing or quoting

        This page is an independent fan-built monitor of the public Figure AI humanoid-robot livestream on YouTube. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Figure AI Inc. The video stream is the property of Figure AI and is embedded here only via YouTube's standard public-embed mechanism; we do not redistribute, record, or relay the video itself.

        All numbers, rates, trends, segment narratives, time-of-day patterns, predictions, and verdicts shown above are produced by an autonomous AI vision pipeline (currently gpt-5.4-nano reading single frames every ~60 seconds) plus deterministic post-processing. Vision OCR can mis-read digits, mis-identify robots, or skip frames. Calculations are estimates for entertainment and educational purposes only and should not be used for any commercial, financial, contractual, journalistic, or operational decision.

        Continuous development

        The site is in permanent active development. Features change daily; old verdicts may be revised as the pipeline improves. The exact formulas and source code evolve — what you see today may differ tomorrow.

        Calculations may be wrong

        OCR mis-reads happen. Sanity validators reject obviously-bad readings but subtle errors can slip through. Every metric carries implicit ±5–15% uncertainty; confidence levels are surfaced on the verdict card and the audit page.

        Autonomous AI processing

        Vision frames are sent to an OpenAI-compatible LLM gateway. Token usage and cost are tracked transparently in the "OCR Cost Tracking" panel. No human reviews each reading; the pipeline runs unattended.

        Not affiliated · not financial advice

        This project is a personal technical exercise. Nothing here is investment advice, a recommendation, a benchmark for any commercial product, or a measurement Figure AI Inc. would necessarily agree with.

        Data & privacy

        The site stores only what's visible on the public livestream (package counts, robot identities visible on chest stickers, timer readings). No personal information about viewers is collected. The dashboard has no analytics scripts beyond what YouTube embeds.

        Trademarks

        "Figure" and "F.03" are trademarks of Figure AI Inc. They are used here for descriptive and fair-use purposes only. All other names (ROSE, GARY, JIM, BOB, FRANK …) are identifiers we observed on the public stream.
